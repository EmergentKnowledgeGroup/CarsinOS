# Code of Conduct

CarsinOS is built for people who care about useful, accountable local AI.
Treat other contributors with respect. Critique code, evidence, and ideas—not
people. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, dox, or publish another
person’s private information.

If conduct creates a safety concern, contact the maintainers privately through
the repository’s security-reporting channel. Maintainers may remove content or
limit participation when necessary to keep the project constructive and safe.
